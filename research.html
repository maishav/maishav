Environmental Economics Behaviour Research Under Review

Assessing Household Waste Management Behaviour through a Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice (KAP) Lens: Case Study of a Deltaic Urban City in Bangladesh

Abstract

This study examines the gap between what households know, believe, and actually do when it comes to waste management in Barishal City, Bangladesh — a fast-growing deltaic urban area. Drawing on survey data from 431 households, it finds that while most residents hold positive attitudes toward proper waste disposal, far fewer translate that into consistent practice, and identifies what actually predicts good practice, from education to community awareness campaigns. The findings offer practical direction for waste policy in similarly vulnerable urban centres, tying into SDG 11 and SDG 12.

Methodology

KAP Scoring

02
Development Economics Food Security Under Review

Assessing Food Security and Household Coping Strategies in Coastal Bhola: An Empirical Study

Abstract

This study looks at food security and how households cope in coastal Bhola, one of Bangladesh's most climate-vulnerable districts. Drawing on survey data from 400 households, it identifies which socioeconomic factors put families at greater risk of food insecurity and what coping strategies they turn to when access is threatened. The findings point toward more targeted nutrition and livelihood support for coastal communities.
