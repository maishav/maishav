Selected Research

Where my work lives.

Primary research on food, environment, and economic behaviour in coastal Bangladesh.

Waste Management · Behaviour
STATA 14.0 NVivo 15 431 Households

Assessing Waste Management Behaviour through a KAP Lens: Coastal Urban Households in Bangladesh

Applied a Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice framework to understand household waste behaviours across coastal urban settlements. Used linear regression, logistic regression, chi-square tests, and thematic analysis.

Food Security · Coastal
STATA 14.0 400 Households Poisson Regression

Assessing Food Security and Household Coping Strategies in Coastal Bhola: An Empirical Study

Primary data from 400 households in coastal Bhola analysed using logistic and Poisson regression to map food insecurity patterns and identify key socioeconomic drivers of coping strategies.
